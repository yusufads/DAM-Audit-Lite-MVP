# DAM Signals v1.0

## Purpose

DAM Signals define the measurable perception indicators used by the GEO-DAM Framework.

Each signal represents a specific component of Digital Perception Readiness.

Signals are grouped into five dimensions:

* Entity Presence
* Authority Signals
* Knowledge Layer
* AI Visibility
* Perception Layer

Signals are considered stable framework components.

Questions may evolve over time, but signals should remain consistent whenever possible.

---

# Entity Presence

Measures whether a brand exists as a clear, identifiable and connected digital entity.

## entity_foundation

The existence of a clear and authoritative digital home for the brand.

## brand_identity

The ability of users and AI systems to understand who the brand is and what it does.

## expert_association

The visibility of founders, experts or identifiable people associated with the brand.

## cross_platform_consistency

The consistency of the brand across websites, social media and external platforms.

## business_legitimacy

The availability of business information that supports trust and legitimacy.

---

# Authority Signals

Measures whether the brand demonstrates credibility through external validation.

## external_validation

Evidence that third-party sources acknowledge the existence of the brand.

## citation_strength

The presence of citations, mentions and references from trusted sources.

## media_presence

Visibility within news, media and industry publications.

## expert_authority

Recognition of the brand through expertise, thought leadership and professional contributions.

## industry_recognition

Recognition within the brand's professional or commercial ecosystem.

---

# Knowledge Layer

Measures whether the brand publishes useful and machine-readable knowledge.

## knowledge_depth

The depth and completeness of informational content.

## faq_coverage

The ability to answer common customer and industry questions.

## semantic_structure

The organization of information using logical and machine-understandable structures.

## content_authority

The demonstration of expertise through educational content.

## machine_readability

The degree to which information can be interpreted by search engines and AI systems.

---

# AI Visibility

Measures whether a brand is visible and correctly represented within AI environments.

## ai_discoverability

The ability of AI systems to find and reference the brand.

## ai_accuracy

The accuracy of AI-generated descriptions about the brand.

## ai_association

The ability of AI systems to associate the brand with relevant topics and expertise areas.

## ai_recommendation

The likelihood of the brand being recommended in relevant AI-generated answers.

## ai_optimization

The level of intentional optimization for AI search and generative discovery.

---

# Perception Layer

Measures how the brand is perceived by users and AI systems.

## trust_signals

The visibility of trust-building elements such as reviews, testimonials and credentials.

## proof_signals

The presence of evidence supporting competence and results.

## differentiation

The ability to communicate what makes the brand unique.

## positioning_clarity

The clarity of the brand's market position and value proposition.

## reputation_consistency

The consistency of perception across digital touchpoints.

---
