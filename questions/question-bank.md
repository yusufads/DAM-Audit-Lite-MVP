# DAM Audit Lite Question Bank v1.0

## Purpose

This document contains the official question bank used by DAM Audit Lite.

Each question is mapped to a specific GEO-DAM signal.

The purpose of the question bank is to:

* Measure Digital Perception Readiness
* Support DAM Score calculation
* Feed the Digital Perception Dataset
* Serve as the source for future JSON implementations

---

# Entity Presence

## EP1

Signal:
entity_foundation

Question:

Does your brand have a clearly identifiable official website that serves as its primary digital source?

Why It Matters:

AI systems require a trusted primary source to understand, validate and connect information about a brand.

---

## EP2

Signal:
brand_identity

Question:

Can a first-time visitor immediately understand who your brand is, what it does and who it serves?

Why It Matters:

Clear brand identity improves understanding for both users and AI systems.

---

## EP3

Signal:
expert_association

Question:

Are identifiable experts, founders or representatives publicly associated with your brand?

Why It Matters:

Visible expertise strengthens trust, authority and entity recognition.

---

## EP4

Signal:
cross_platform_consistency

Question:

Does your brand maintain a consistent identity across websites, social profiles and external platforms?

Why It Matters:

Consistency helps AI systems connect multiple signals into a single entity.

---

## EP5

Signal:
business_legitimacy

Question:

Can users easily verify your business through contact details, company information and public trust indicators?

Why It Matters:

Verification signals improve legitimacy, trust and confidence.

# AI Visibility

## AV1

Signal:
ai_discoverability

Question:

Can AI systems such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Perplexity reliably discover your brand when its name is searched?

Why It Matters:

A brand cannot be recommended if it cannot first be discovered and identified by AI systems.

---

## AV2

Signal:
ai_accuracy

Question:

When AI systems describe your brand, is the information accurate, current and aligned with reality?

Why It Matters:

Incorrect AI-generated information can damage trust, reputation and customer confidence.

---

## AV3

Signal:
ai_association

Question:

Do AI systems associate your brand with the products, services, expertise and topics you want to be known for?

Why It Matters:

Brands are increasingly discovered through topic association rather than direct brand searches.

---

## AV4

Signal:
ai_recommendation

Question:

Can your brand appear as a recommendation when users ask AI systems for solutions within your category?

Why It Matters:

AI recommendation visibility is one of the strongest indicators of future digital discoverability.

---

## AV5

Signal:
ai_optimization

Question:

Has your brand intentionally implemented strategies to improve visibility in AI-driven search and generative discovery environments?

Why It Matters:

Brands that actively optimize for AI systems are more likely to gain future visibility advantages.
