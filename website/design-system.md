# DAM Audit Lite Design System v1.0

## Design Philosophy

DAM Audit Lite should feel like an AI-native intelligence platform.

The experience should communicate:

* Trust
* Clarity
* Intelligence
* Simplicity

Avoid:

* Corporate consulting aesthetics
* Traditional SEO tool interfaces
* Overly technical dashboards

The product should feel closer to:

* Perplexity
* Linear
* Notion
* Stripe

Than:

* Legacy SEO software
* Enterprise reporting tools

---

# Brand Position

Category:

AI Visibility Assessment Platform

Core Promise:

Understand how AI systems perceive your brand.

Tone:

* Intelligent
* Direct
* Credible
* Modern

Avoid:

* Marketing hype
