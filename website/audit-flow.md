# DAM Audit Lite Audit Flow v1.0

## Purpose

This document defines the assessment experience used by DAM Audit Lite.

The primary goal is to maximize audit completion rates while collecting high-quality Digital Perception data.

Target Completion Rate:

70%+

Target Completion Time:

3–5 Minutes

---

# Flow Overview

Landing Page

↓

Brand Information

↓

Question Groups

↓

Score Preview

↓

Email Capture

↓

Success Page

# Step 1 — Brand Information

## Goal

* Industry

* Country

# Answer Model

Each question uses:

* No
* Partially
* Yes

Values:

0
2
4

Display:

Large clickable cards.

Avoid:

Dropdowns
Radio buttons
Small controls

---

# Score Generation

After Question 25:

Calculate:

* Total DAM Score
* Category Scores
* Critical Signals

Do NOT show recommendations yet.

---

# Score Preview Page

## Goal

Generate curiosity.

Display:

* DAM Score
* Category Scores
* Score Classification

Hide:

* Findings
* Recommendations
* Roadmap

Example Message:

Your brand shows strong Digital Perception potential but several critical AI visibility signals require improvement.

---

# UX Principles

## Principle 1

One decision per screen.

Avoid overwhelming users.

---

## Principle 2

Show progress continuously.

---

## Principle 3

Reveal score before email capture.

---

## Principle 4

Hide recommendations until email submission.

---

## Principle 5

Optimize for completion, not data collection.

Collect only essential information.
