# DAM Audit Lite Assessment Page Specification v1.0

## Purpose

The Assessment Page collects Digital Perception signals while maintaining a high completion rate.

Target Completion Rate:

70%+

Target Duration:

3–5 Minutes

---

# Design Goal

The assessment should feel:

* Fast
* Intelligent
* Valuable

It should NOT feel:

* Like a survey
* Like a form
* Like homework

---

# Page Structure

Progress Bar

↓

Current Category

↓

Question

↓

Answer Cards

↓

Continue

---

# Header

Display:

DAM Audit Lite

Powered by GEO-DAM

Progress:

40%

---

# Category Indicator

Example:

Authority Signals

Question 7 of 25

---

# Question Area

Display only ONE question at a time.

Example:

Has your brand been referenced by independent third-party sources?

---

# Answer Model

Three large cards:

NO

PARTIALLY

YES

Values:

0
2
4

---

# UX Rule

Answers must be selectable with a single click.

No dropdowns.

No radio buttons.

No small controls.

---

# Progress Bar

Always visible.

Purpose:

Reduce abandonment.

---

After each category:

Display:

# Mobile Rules

# Completion Psychology

Users should feel:

"I am discovering something about my brand."

Not:

"I am filling out a questionnaire."
