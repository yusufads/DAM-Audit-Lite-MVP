# DAM Audit Lite UI Wireframe v1.0

## Design Philosophy

DAM Audit Lite should feel like an AI-native assessment platform.

The experience should resemble:

* Perplexity
* Linear
* Notion
* Stripe

Not traditional audit software.

---

# Design Principles

## Principle 1

Clarity Over Complexity

One action per screen.

---

## Principle 2

Mobile First

Most audits should be completable from a mobile device.

---

## Principle 3

Fast Perception

Users should understand what to do within 3 seconds.

---

## Principle 4

Progress Visibility

Users must always know where they are in the assessment.

---

## Principle 5

AI Native Feel

Modern typography.

Minimal interface.

No corporate dashboard aesthetics.
